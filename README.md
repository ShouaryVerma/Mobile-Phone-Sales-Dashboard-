# Mobile-Phone-Sales-Dashboard-
An interactive Power BI dashboard that provides a complete view of mobile phone sales performance — covering sales trends, product performance, regional distribution, payment behavior, and customer segmentation.

Overview

This dashboard analyzes mobile phone sales data from 9 Oct 2021 to 8 Oct 2024, giving a 360° view of revenue, units sold, customer behavior, and payment trends across five brands (Apple, Samsung, OnePlus, Vivo, Xiaomi) and multiple regions/cities in India.

📊 Dashboard Pages

The report is organized into 6 pages, navigable from the left sidebar:

1. Overview

Landing page with headline KPIs (Total Sales, Units Sold, Transactions, Avg. Selling Price, Total Customers) and quick navigation cards into each detailed section.

2. Sales Analysis
Sales trend over time (monthly)
Sales by price range (donut)
Sales by brand (bar chart)
Units sold & sales by payment method
Mobile model–level sales table (Brand, Units Sold, Total Sales, Avg. Selling Price, % of Total)
3. Product Analysis
Sales & units sold by brand
Sales by price range
Sales by payment method and by city
Top performing brand: Apple, across 15 unique models
Sales trend by price range over time
4. Regional Sales
Sales by region (North, West, South, East, Central)
Sales trend by region (monthly)
Sales distribution donut
Key insight: North is the top-performing region, contributing 42.93% of total sales; Central contributes the least
5. Payment Analysis
Sales & units sold by payment method (UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card, Cash)
Payment method trend over time
Transaction-level breakdown table with avg. order value and sales %
Key insight: UPI is the highest-value payment method; payment methods are fairly evenly balanced (~24–26% share each)
6. Customer Analysis
Repeat customer rate: 91.1%
New vs. returning customers (88 new / 900 returning)
Customer segmentation: High Value (76.42%), Regular (67.71%), Occasional (14.68%), New (8.91%)
Customer distribution by city (Delhi, Mumbai, Ranchi, Hyderabad)
Top customers table by total sales and transaction count

🚀 Getting Started
Install Power BI Desktop (free).
Open mobile_sales.pbix.
If prompted, update the data source connection to point to your dataset.
Use the left-hand navigation pane to move between report pages.
Use the date range slider at the top of each page to filter by time period.
📌 Key Insights
Apple leads as the top-performing brand by sales.
Phones priced above ₹50,000 account for nearly half (49.96%) of total sales.
North region drives the highest share of revenue (42.93%).
UPI and Debit Card together account for over half of all payment volume.
The customer base is highly loyal, with a 91.1% repeat purchase rate.
